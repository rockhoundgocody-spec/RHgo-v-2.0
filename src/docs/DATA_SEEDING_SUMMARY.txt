  TOP 10 SEED MINERALS (MVP)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

 1. Quartz (SiO₂) — Most abundant, pegmatite + veins, hardness 7
 2. Feldspar (KAlSi₃O₈) — Common, granite, softer than quartz (hardness 6)
 3. Mica (Muscovite) — Pegmatite indicator, splits into sheets (hardness 2-3)
 4. Tourmaline — Collector favorite, pegmatite, colorful (hardness 7-7.5)
 5. Beryl — Gem mineral, pegmatite, hardness 7.5-8
 6. Pyrite — "Fool's gold", cubic crystals, metallic luster
 7. Magnetite — Iron oxide, responds to magnet, black metallic
 8. Fluorite — Colorful, cubic cleavage, fluoresces under UV
 9. Calcite — Fizzes in HCl, soft (hardness 3), common
10. Malachite — Striking green, banded, secondary copper mineral
